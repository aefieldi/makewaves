✨ TikTok LIVE resources

How to run your LIVE

The flow I use on my own LIVEs, from the video before you go on to the moments when the room is quiet.

🎬
5 minutes before

Post a video first

Post a video about 5 minutes before you go LIVE. It pushes you out in the algorithm, so more people find your LIVE when it starts.

🔮
When you open

Open with a reading or your story

Start with a card reading or by sharing your story, and welcome people into the room as they arrive.

🔁
Every 5 to 10 minutes

Repeat your intro

People come in and out of your LIVE all the time. Say who you are and what you're doing again, so the people who just arrived know why to stay.

🌊
When the room is empty

Keep talking to the algorithm

It's totally normal for no one to be in your room at first. When you're talking, you're telling the algorithm who to send to your room. The more you repeat your keywords, the more you're telling it what to do.
